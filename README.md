## ✨ Key Gameplay Highlights

### ✈️ Realistic Aviation, Six-Pack Cockpit HUD & Radar Stealth
- **Coordinated Bank-to-Turn Flight Physics**: Smooth aerodynamic angle-of-attack inertia, throttle spooling, runway takeoff roll, and progressive stall recovery.
- **Collision Avoidance (`AUTO ON` vs. Realistic Crash Mode `[G]`)**: Defaults to beginner-friendly terrain/building pull-up assistance (`AUTO ON`), or toggle **`[G]`** (or Settings) for realistic structure/ground/mountain impact explosions.
- **Nap-of-the-Earth Radar Ground-Clutter Stealth (`< 28m AGL`)**: Flying treetop-low below `28m AGL` masks your aircraft in terrain radar clutter—hiding your radar Target Designator box, preventing Stinger/SPAAG/A2A locks, and breaking incoming homing missiles!
- **3D Angel-Wing Flare Countermeasures (`[X]`) & WebAudio Jet Engines**: Eject glowing 3D twin-wing magnesium IR flare arcs with smoke trails while dynamic WebAudio synthesizes turbofan compressor whine, afterburner roar (`[W]`), AC-130 turboprop drone, and Doppler flybys.
- **360m Unobstructed Airfields**: Combat aircraft park in side flight-line apron bays with yellow taxi lead-in stripes, keeping the entire `360m` main runway clear for takeoffs and landings.

### 🛡️ Armored Warfare & Destructible Voxel Buildings
- **Directional Tank Hull Integrity & ERA Tiles**: Main Battle Tanks model **Front Glacis (`35%` deflection)**, **Left/Right Skirts**, and **Rear Engine Deck (`1.45×` critical)** armor zones, **Explosive Reactive Armor (ERA)** blocks, and subsystem damage (degraded track speed, turret traverse, and progressive engine smoke/fire).
- **1-Block Obstacle Crushing & Climbing**: Tanks and ground vehicles crush destructible low cover and physically pitch/climb right over indestructible 1-block obstacles.
- **Progressive Building Collapse & Repair**: Tank shells, RPGs, C4, and trebuchet boulders carve 3D craters into multi-story buildings; Engineers and players can repair damaged forward-base buildings (`[F]` / `[4]`).

### ⚔️ Mount & Blade Directional Melee, Shields & Cavalry Trampling
- **4-Direction Zweihänder Combat**: Swing direction (`Overhead Chop`, `Left/Right Horizontal Cleave`, `Step-In Lunge Thrust`) follows your mouse movement with authentic Mordhau/Half-Sword crossguard parries (`RMB`).
- **Active & Passive Shield Coverage**: `1H Heater Shields` and `2H Pavise Shields` physically intercept arrows and melee strikes (`70%–92%` mitigation) with wood-and-iron impact audio and stamina stagger feedback.
- **Mass-Inertia Physics & Warhorse Trampling**: Soldiers, horses, motorcycles, tanks, and jets accelerate progressively through realistic mass/gear curves; galloping warhorses trample and knock back enemy foot soldiers.

### ★ Force Commander Orders, Gunsmith Armory & Tarkov Loot Extraction
- **Command All 5 Squads (`125 Friendly AI`) or Your Squad (`25 AI`)**: Issue **`⚔️ CHARGE [Alt+C]`**, **`🛡️ DEFEND / SHIELD WALL [Alt+H]`**, **`🏳️ RETREAT [Alt+R]`**, **`🏃 FOLLOW [Alt+F]`**, **`🏰 GARRISON ON/OFF [Alt+G]`**, or **`🤖 AI ON/OFF [Alt+A]`** via the HUD panel, **`[Y]`** Radial Wheel, or **`[K]`** Tactical Map.
- **2D Grid Backpack (`[I]`), Gunsmith Workbench (`[L]`) & CS2 Case Roulette**: Loot fallen enemies, pack loose rounds into magazines (`[P]`), customize optics/silencers/ammunition, find golden Armory Keys inside buildings, and spin animated weapon skin cases.

## 🚀 Play Online or Run Locally

### 1. Play Instantly in Your Browser (No Install)
Open **[https://blockbuilderb.github.io/blockfare/](https://blockbuilderb.github.io/blockfare/)** in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

### 2. Run Locally (with Full LAN / Online WebSocket Multiplayer Rooms)
Requires only **Python 3.8+** (100% standard library—zero `pip` or `npm` install required):

```bash
git clone https://github.com/blockbuilderb/blockfare.git
cd blockfare
python server.py
```
Then open **`http://localhost:8080`** in your browser. Press **`[O]`** (or switch from **Solo** to **Co-Op** on the Deploy Screen) to share a live LAN/tunnel invite link across rooms (`FRUGIS-1`, `ALPHA-SQUAD`, `ARMOR-WAR`).
